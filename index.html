Dinner by 5:30

A 45-minute cook starts at 4:45, so the decision can't happen at 4:45. Make it Sunday.

  • Sunday: pick five dinners, shop once. A rotation of 8–10 meals is plenty.
  • Weeknights are 30–40 minute meals: sheet-pan, one-pot, stir-fry, pasta, tacos.
  • Two nights a week cook double; eat it again two days later.
  • Keep a freezer backstop (soup, a sauce, dumplings) for the day the torch runs late.
  • Phone alarms: 4:30 "close studio", 4:45 "start dinner". The alarms remember so you don't have to.
